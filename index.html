Privacy Policy

Your data. Your device. Your control.

Last updated: October 13, 2026
🛡️

Stiqo is built with a privacy-first approach. Your habits, streaks, and personal data never leave your device. No accounts. No cloud. No tracking. Ever.

1

Information We Collect

Absolutely nothing. Stiqo does not collect any personal information, usage data, analytics, or device identifiers. The app operates entirely offline on your device.

2

Data Storage

All data you create within Stiqo — habits, streaks, notes, and settings — is stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export a backup file.

3

Third-Party Services

Stiqo does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

🚫 No Ads 🚫 No Analytics 🚫 No Tracking 🚫 No Cloud Sync 🚫 No Social SDKs
4

Permissions

Stiqo may request the following device permissions, used solely for their stated purposes:

🔔
Notifications
To send habit reminders you configure
⏰
Exact Alarms
To schedule reminders at specific times
📁
Storage (optional)
Only when you export or import backup files
5

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user data with third parties. Since no data is collected, there is nothing to share.

6

Children's Privacy

Stiqo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7

Data Deletion
