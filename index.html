🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Stiqo ("we", "our", or "the app") is a private habit tracker built with your privacy as the top priority. This policy explains how we handle your information.

✅ Stiqo does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All your data stays 100% on your device.

1. Information We Collect

None. Stiqo does not collect any personal information, usage data, analytics, or device identifiers. The app works entirely offline.

2. Data Storage

All data you create within Stiqo (habits, streaks, notes, settings) is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

3. Third-Party Services

Stiqo does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app contains:

  • No ads
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No social media integrations
  • No cloud syncing

4. Permissions

Stiqo may request the following device permissions:

  • Notifications: To send you habit reminders that you configure
  • Exact Alarms: To schedule reminders at specific times
  • Storage (optional): Only when you choose to export/import backup files

These permissions are used solely for the stated purposes and do not involve any data transmission.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user data with third parties. Since no data is collected, there is nothing to share.

6. Children's Privacy

Stiqo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by:

  • Using the "Clear Data" option in your device's App Settings
  • Uninstalling the app

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
